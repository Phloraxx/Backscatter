# Glossary

**SAR** — Synthetic Aperture Radar.

**Backscatter** — radar energy scattered back toward the sensor.

**NISAR** — NASA–ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar mission.

**L-band** — longer-wavelength microwave radar band used by NISAR's NASA instrument.

**S-band** — shorter-wavelength microwave radar band used by NISAR's ISRO instrument.

**Polarization** — orientation of transmitted/received radar fields, e.g. HH/HV depending on mode.

**GCOV** — geocoded polarimetric covariance product.

**GUNW** — geocoded unwrapped interferogram product.

**Coherence** — measure of similarity between complex SAR observations.

**InSAR** — interferometric SAR, using radar phase differences to infer displacement and other effects.

**dB** — decibel scale commonly used for radar backscatter.

**AOI** — area of interest.

**COG** — Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF.

**DEM** — digital elevation model.

**IoU** — intersection over union, a segmentation metric.

**Provisional data** — released calibrated data that may still have incomplete validation / documented limitations.
