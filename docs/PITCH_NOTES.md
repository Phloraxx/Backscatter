# Pitch Notes

## 15-second version

Clouds can hide the ground exactly when floods matter most. Backscatter uses NASA–ISRO NISAR radar observations to measure surface change through cloud cover, quantify probable inundation, and show the evidence behind every detected region.

## Problem

Optical imagery can be blocked by clouds during major weather events. SAR remains useful, but interpreting radar data is difficult for non-specialists.

## Solution

Backscatter transforms repeat NISAR observations into:
- change maps;
- probable inundation masks;
- area metrics;
- confidence;
- evidence and provenance.

## Why NISAR

NISAR is built for repeat, high-quality radar observation of changing Earth systems and is directly relevant to flooding/wetlands and other surface-change use cases.

## Why now

The project is designed around the newly available NISAR data era, not legacy imagery with a NISAR label added afterward.

## Demo close

Do not end on architecture.
End on:
- visible change;
- quantified area;
- validation;
- transferability.
