# Do Not Do

- Do not call the project real-time if it processes fixed acquisitions offline.
- Do not claim centimeter-scale deformation unless the implemented InSAR workflow supports it.
- Do not call a generic dark SAR pixel "water" without calibration/validation.
- Do not hide provisional-data limitations.
- Do not use random AI-generated explanations detached from numerical evidence.
- Do not add ten NASA datasets merely to increase dataset count.
- Do not spend the hackathon building authentication/admin panels.
- Do not build a microservice architecture for three case studies.
- Do not prioritize a 3D globe over fast scientific interaction.
- Do not tune on the validation region and report that metric as independent.
- Do not invent case-study metrics for screenshots.
