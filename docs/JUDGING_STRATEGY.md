# Judging Strategy

The latest published Space Apps judging framework used:
- Impact
- Creativity
- Validity
- Relevance
- Presentation

Treat the exact 2026 wording as subject to official confirmation.

## Target concept score

Planning estimate: **92/100 potential**, contingent on execution.

### Impact — target 18/20
Make the use case concrete:
- cloud-obscured flood/wetland monitoring;
- disaster/ecosystem relevance;
- transferability beyond one region.

### Creativity — target 18/20
Differentiator:
**Change → Evidence → Explanation**

Not just layers.

### Validity — target 17+/20
Must have:
- compatible scene selection;
- reproducible processing;
- uncertainty;
- independent validation;
- no exaggerated claims.

### Relevance — target 20/20
NISAR should be the core measurement system, not a supporting dataset.

### Presentation — target 19/20
The map interaction should make the result obvious in under a minute.

## Award-fit strategy

Natural targets based on recent Space Apps award structures:
- Best Use of Data
- Best Use of Science
- Local Impact
- Best Use of Technology

Do not optimize for award labels at the expense of the core challenge.
